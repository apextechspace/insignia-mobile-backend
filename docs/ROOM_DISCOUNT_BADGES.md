# Room offer badges

Admin → Suites & Rooms → Edit → Pricing & Commercial Terms.

The owner enters the **final nightly price**, including any VAT and discount already calculated by the owner. Guest checkout continues to charge that price multiplied by nights. No automatic VAT, tax changes, promotional deductions, or inferred original prices have been introduced.

“Show discount offer” controls a guest-facing badge. Enter the percentage already reflected in the final price, plus an optional name such as Ramadan Offer or Introductory Offer. Turning the badge off hides it; it does not restore or change the price. To end special pricing, the owner edits the final nightly price as well.

Existing room prices and bookings are not rewritten. New fields default to discount off. Deploy the migration and matching mobile web release, then check a room with the badge on and off. Ensure the displayed rate is the desired final amount before publishing.
